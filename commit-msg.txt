refactor: remove "Dò khớp" mode + deeper AI vocabulary scan + phrase-aware lookup

Three changes per user request (Aug 1 2026):

1. "Dò khớp" mode removed.
   - index.html: removed the "🎯 Dò khớp" button.
   - js/app.js: removed all match-mode state, hover/click handlers, button binding,
     and the `_applyMatchHighlight` DOM logic. `_initMatchMode`, `_setMatchModeActive`,
     `_applyMatchHighlight` are now no-ops so existing call sites don't throw.
   - The user can still hover/click ANY word in reading canvas — the dictionary lookup
     popup will appear (when in "Tra từ" mode).

2. AI vocabulary scan — much deeper, more collocations/phrasal/idioms/vocab.
   - js/translator.js `_scanTextChunkWithAI`: prompt expanded from 6 categories to
     9 categories, explicitly covering collocation_adj_noun, collocation_verb_noun,
     collocation_noun_noun, collocation_adv_adj, collocation_adv_verb, phrasal_verb,
     idiom, grammar, vocabulary. Min/max term counts raised: short chunks now require
     at LEAST 25 items (was 10), medium chunks 50+, capped by natural content.
   - Result: highlightable terms on any English text are now comprehensive — collocations
     (Adj+N, V+N, N+N, Adv+Adj, Adv+V), phrasal verbs, idioms, grammar structures,
     and standout single-word academic vocabulary.

3. Dictionary lookup — phrase-aware, no source sentence, natural example.
   - js/app.js `_getWordAtPoint`: when the hovered word sits INSIDE a contiguous
     highlighted <mark>, the lookup target becomes the WHOLE mark (data-text or its
     visible text), NOT just the single word. E.g. hover "fundamentally" inside the
     highlighted "fundamentally altered" → look up "fundamentally altered". Hover
     "altered" inside the same mark → still "fundamentally altered". Hover an
     UNHIGHLIGHTED word → just that word. This is the user's specific request:
     "dịch cả cụm được tô liền nhau, không dịch cả câu".
   - js/translator.js `lookupWord`: cache key is now `v4|<word>` only (no sentence
     context). Bump invalidates old v3 entries.
   - js/translator.js `_lookupWithAI`: prompt no longer sends CONTEXT. Meaning now
     comes from the most common dictionary sense (Oxford/Cambridge style). Example
     is required to be a NATURAL English sentence containing the word/phrase (like
     something from a real dictionary or published writing) — NOT "Example with X"
     and NOT made-up on the fly. Structures remain as 2-3 collocations/idioms each
     with their own natural example.

Tests: 11/11 highlight.test.js cases still pass.

UX changes:
- "🎯 Dò khớp" button is gone. Only "Bút tô" + "Tra từ" mode buttons remain.
- "Tra từ" mode: hover/click a single word → look up that word. Hover/click a word
  inside a highlighted phrase → look up the WHOLE phrase (data-text).
- "AI Scan" now produces 2-3x more highlightable terms per chunk, covering collocations,
  phrasal verbs, idioms, and standout single-word academic vocabulary.
